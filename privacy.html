Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

JustPICK ("JustPICK," "we," "us," or "our") operates the JustPICK mobile application (the "App"), a meal-decision assistant that helps users decide what to cook or where to eat. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights available to you. By downloading, accessing, or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy applies only to the App and does not apply to third-party websites, products, or services, even those linked to or accessible from within the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide to Us

  • Account information — when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google.
  • Preferences — cuisine preferences, dietary restrictions, allergen exclusions, cooking appliance availability, household size, and other settings you enter to personalize recommendations.
  • Photos you submit — photos you take for Cooking Streak check-ins, and photos you voluntarily submit of restaurant menus, which may be reviewed by us and displayed to other users after moderation.
  • Saved content — recipes, restaurants, and shopping list items you save within the App.
  • Communications — if you contact us for support, we collect the content of your message and your contact information.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data — features used, screens viewed, session length, and interaction patterns (for example, meals rejected or recipes generated).
  • Device data — device model, operating system and version, unique device identifiers, and app version, collected for crash reporting and troubleshooting.
  • Approximate or precise location — when you use Eat Out Mode, we access your device location (depending on the permission you grant) to find nearby restaurants via the Google Places API. Location is not collected when Eat Out Mode is not in use.

1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not knowingly collect government identification numbers, financial account details, or precise health or medical records. Allergen and dietary preferences you enter are self-reported and used solely to filter recommendations within the App — see Section 8.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and personalize the App's core features (Cook at Home, Eat Out, Feast Mode, Saved Meals, Shopping List, Cooking Streak);
  • Generate AI-powered meal, recipe, and restaurant recommendations;
  • Apply your allergen and dietary exclusion settings to recommendations;
  • Maintain your account, preferences, and saved content across sessions and devices;
  • Review and moderate user-submitted restaurant menu photos before they are shown to other users;
  • Diagnose bugs, monitor performance, and improve the App;
  • Communicate with you about your account, support requests, or material changes to our policies;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use your information to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

JustPICK is operated from Canada and processes personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation. We collect, use, and disclose personal information only with your knowledge and consent — express or implied through your use of the App — except where permitted or required by law. If you are located outside Canada, we process your information based on your consent, to perform our contract with you (providing the App's services), and, where applicable, to comply with legal obligations.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party service providers to operate the App. Each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles your data:

  • Google Sign-In — authentication (name, email, profile photo).
  • Supabase — backend database, authentication, and file storage, including menu and check-in photos.
  • Google Places API / Geocoding API — restaurant search and location-based recommendations.
  • Google Gemini API — AI-generated recipe, meal, and restaurant recommendations, accessed via a proxied Supabase Edge Function; your prompts and preferences may be sent to Google's Gemini API for processing.
  • Google AdMob — advertising, once enabled (see Section 11).
  • RevenueCat — subscription and in-app purchase management, once paid features launch.
  • Instacart, Uber Eats, DoorDash, Amazon Associates — affiliate links that may appear within the App; clicking these links takes you to a third-party site subject to that party's own privacy practices.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide the App's services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. Menu photos you submit that have already been published for other users may be retained after account deletion, with directly identifying information removed.

6. Data Storage and International Transfers

Your information is stored using Supabase and Google Cloud infrastructure, which may store or process data outside of Canada, including in the United States. By using the App, you consent to this transfer, storage, and processing of your information outside your country of residence. We take reasonable steps to ensure your information receives an adequate level of protection wherever it is processed.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable organizational and technical safeguards designed to protect your personal information, including encrypted storage and access controls. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your device and account credentials secure.

8. Allergen and Dietary Information

The allergen exclusion and dietary preference settings you provide are used only to filter and generate recommendations within the App. This information is self-reported by you and is not medical or health data verified by a professional. JustPICK's allergen filtering is a convenience feature, not a medical safety system — see the "Allergen and Dietary Disclaimer" in our Terms of Service for important limitations. We do not share your allergen or dietary information with third parties except the service providers listed in Section 4, solely as needed to generate your recommendations.

9. Children's Privacy

JustPICK is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without appropriate consent, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided us with personal information should contact us at the address below.

10. Advertising and Analytics (Future)

We currently do not display third-party advertisements. Once we enable Google AdMob, this Policy will be updated to describe the advertising identifiers and data used for ad personalization, and you will be able to manage ad personalization through your device settings. We will provide notice of this change per Section 12.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Request deletion of your personal information;
  • Withdraw consent to our processing of your information, which may limit your ability to use certain features;
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@getjustpick.app. We will respond within a reasonable time and in accordance with applicable law. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) or your local data protection authority.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide additional notice within the App, such as a prompt on next launch.
