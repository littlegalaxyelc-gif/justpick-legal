Delete Your Account

JustPICK

You can request deletion of your JustPICK account and associated data at any time, directly from within the app or by contacting us.

How to request deletion

Email privacy@getjustpick.app from the email address linked to your JustPICK account, with the subject line "Delete my account". Include the account email so we can locate and verify your account before processing the request.

What gets deleted

Once we verify your request, we delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, including your account profile, saved preferences, saved recipes/restaurants, shopping lists, and Cooking Streak check-in photos.

What may be retained

Menu photos you submitted that have already been published for other users may be retained after account deletion, with directly identifying information removed. We may also retain limited information where required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

Timeline

Deletion requests are typically processed within a reasonable period of verification. If you have questions about the status of your request, contact privacy@getjustpick.app.

See our Privacy Policy for more detail on data retention.
